This site has no account-support service, payment desk or private message form. We cannot issue tokens, restore access, choose winners or reverse charges. Never send us a password, one-time code, reset link, identity document, card number or wallet recovery phrase.

Report an editorial error

The repository has GitHub issues enabled. You can open a public editorial issue about a broken link, unclear passage or factual correction. GitHub may require an account. This is a public channel operated under GitHub's own terms and privacy policy, not a confidential support inbox.

Include the page address, section heading, the statement you believe is wrong and an official source supporting a correction. A short description is enough. Do not attach screenshots of private rooms, account history, messages, identity checks or financial records. Publication of a report does not guarantee a response time or a particular editorial outcome.

Account and billing questions

Navigate to support from the official platform you use. For an unrecognized charge, contact the payment provider or issuer through a verified route. If you suspect account compromise, use a trusted device, secure the recovery email and change reused passwords before discussing details publicly.

If a person contacts you claiming to be this site's recovery agent, do not provide credentials or money. OpenRoom has no such role. A public issue is suitable for a typo or link correction, not incident response or a private account dispute.

Private matters

No verified private editorial inbox has been configured for this edition. Do not submit a privacy-sensitive complaint through a public issue. The publisher must supply an appropriate private contact and legal identity before a production launch that requires them. Consult the Privacy Policy for the current site's limited data behavior.
